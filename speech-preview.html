Before — speech drowns in the narration

"It is incredibly fulfilling," he admitted, his voice carrying a genuine warmth that surprised even himself. He carefully placed the newly forged piece on a clean section of the workbench, admiring its dark gleam under the fading light.

Gorm clapped his hands together once, a sharp sound that broke the moment of quiet awe. "Fulfilling? Aye. But fulfillment only gets you so far when Vaelros's armies are marching on our gates. We need more than good swords, friend. We need a miracle wrapped in steel."
